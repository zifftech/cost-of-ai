📖 Methodology & Citations
Core Methodology

This calculator uses peer-reviewed research and industry standards to estimate the environmental impact of AI token processing.

1. Energy Consumption

Base Factor: 0.00000106 kWh per token

Formula: Energy (kWh) = Tokens × Base_Energy_Factor × Model_Factor × Hardware_Factor × PUE_Factor

Source: MIT "From Words to Watts" paper (arXiv:2310.03003)

  • Paper: "From Words to Watts: Benchmarking the Energy Costs of Large Language Model Inference"
  • Authors: Siddharth Samsi, Dan Zhao, et al. (MIT Lincoln Laboratory)
  • Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.03003
2. Carbon Emissions

Carbon Intensity: 0.367 kg CO₂ per kWh (location-based, US average)

Formula: CO₂ (kg) = Energy (kWh) × Carbon_Intensity_Factor

Source: U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) 2023 data

  • Link: https://www.eia.gov/tools/faqs/faq.php?id=74&t=11
  • Note: Carbon intensity varies by region and accounting method (location-based vs. market-based)
3. Water Usage

Base Factor: 0.0000026 L per token

Formula: Water (L) = Tokens × Base_Water_Factor × Model_Factor × Hardware_Factor

Source: Google's comprehensive AI environmental impact study

  • Paper: "Measuring the environmental impact of delivering AI at Google Scale"
  • Link: https://arxiv.org/html/2508.15734v1
Provider-Specific Factors
